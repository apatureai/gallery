The product

Three questions, answered automatically after every release.

Meridian ingests your deploy events and your existing telemetry. It does not ask you to instrument anything new.

Did anything regress?

Every deploy is scored against the fifteen minutes before it. Latency, error rate, saturation and business metrics are compared with a change-point test, not a threshold you have to tune.

Which change caused it?

Regressions are attributed to a commit range, an owning team and a service, so the page goes to the person who can act instead of to everyone on rotation.

What should we do now?

Roll back, hold the rollout, or ship on. Meridian writes the recommendation into the pull request and into your incident channel with the evidence attached.
